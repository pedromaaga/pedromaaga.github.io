Hackathon parking availability prediction

Developed during the largest student hackathon in Portugal, presented in Lisbon and awarded 4th place. The application forecasts parking availability for specific locations, dates and times across the city.

The solution combines sensor data, weather signals, event schedules and temporal traffic patterns to estimate parking demand with a confidence percentage. Predictions were produced using both AI-driven models and classic regression techniques.

Lisbon hackathon entry with 4th place finish

Multi-source data analysis including weather, events and availability

Hybrid AI and regression modeling for probabilistic forecasts

City-level predictions for location, time and expected occupancy

The project demonstrated how data science and predictive analytics can help drivers choose the best parking options and reduce time spent searching for spots in a busy urban environment.
